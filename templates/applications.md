# Applications

One row per application, newest first. Update the row whenever anything changes.

Statuses:
- **drafted**: CV and form ready, not sent yet.
- **applied**: sent (by the user, or by an agency).
- **screening**: recruiter or HR call stage.
- **interviewing**: interviews or tests in progress (note the stage).
- **offer**: an offer has been made.
- **rejected**: the employer said no.
- **withdrawn**: the user pulled out.
- **closed (no response)**: gone quiet for weeks. Treated as dead, but don't let an agency resubmit.
