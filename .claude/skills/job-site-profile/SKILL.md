# Job site profiles

Read `reference/browser.md`, `sites/<site>.md` if it exists, `workspace/cv.md`, `workspace/profile.md` and `workspace/voice.md`.

1. Open the site in a new tab. If the user isn't signed in, or has no account, ask them to sign in or sign up themselves. You don't create accounts or type passwords. Wait until they say they're in.
2. Before changing anything, look at the existing profile and tell the user what's there and what you plan to change.
3. **Ask before changing visibility settings:** "open to work" banners, recruiter visibility, public or private profile, sharing with employers. These are the user's call and can be seen by their current employer.
4. Fill in the profile from the master CV and profile: headline, summary (in the user's voice, written for the site, not pasted from the CV), roles with dates and short descriptions, skills, education, desired roles, locations, salary and work type. Upload the master CV PDF from `workspace/export/` where the site takes one.
5. Leave terms, consent, marketing and data-sharing boxes for the user, and list them.
6. Save each section as you go if the site asks for it. Read the label of every button first; "Save" is fine, "Apply" or "Send" is not.
7. Report what you changed and what's left for them. Add the site to the profile's site list and record anything learned about the site in `sites/<site>.md`. Commit.
