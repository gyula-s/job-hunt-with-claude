# Greenhouse, Workable, Lever and similar

Single-page forms, usually reached from a job board's "Apply on company site" link. No site-specific tricks recorded yet. Add them here as you find them.

- Read the whole page first: required custom questions often sit below the standard fields.
- Some systems parse an uploaded CV and prefill fields. Check what they filled.
- Equality and demographic questions often come in a separate, voluntary section at the end. Use the user's saved answers or "prefer not to say".
- When LinkedIn Easy Apply shows "via Workable", the questions still come from the employer. Answer them in the user's voice.
